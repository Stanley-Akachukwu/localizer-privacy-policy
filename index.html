Localizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nedum Infotech ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Localizer mobile application ("Localizer" or the "App").

1. Information We Handle

Account information: When you create or use an account, we may process information you provide, such as your name, email address, profile details, authentication information, and account preferences. The exact information depends on the features you use and the App's implementation.

User content: Depending on the features you use, Localizer may process videos, recordings, images, captions, comments, replies, and other content you create or share, along with information needed to associate that content with your account.

Device permissions: Features that record or create media may require camera or microphone access. Screen recording or other capture features may also process the content captured when you choose to use them. You can manage permissions through Android settings, although denying a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

Technical information: The App and its services may process technical information such as app version, operating system, device information, IP address, diagnostic information, and server logs for service operation, security, and troubleshooting. The actual information collected depends on the deployed App, backend, hosting configuration, and integrated third-party tools.

Subscriptions and transactions: If subscription or payment features are available, information related to a plan, subscription status, transaction reference, payment verification, renewal, or expiry may be processed. The payment provider and the information it receives will depend on the payment integration actually used. We will update this policy when that integration is confirmed.

Support messages: If you contact us, we process the information you choose to include so that we can respond.

2. How We Use Information

Depending on the features available and used, information may be used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide media and social features, store or share user content, administer subscriptions, respond to support requests, troubleshoot problems, protect the service against misuse, and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing and Visibility

The visibility of videos, recordings, profile information, and other content depends on the feature and the sharing settings available in Localizer. Content you choose to share with other users may be visible to its intended audience. Consider the audience and settings before sharing content.

We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the App, such as backend hosting, databases, storage, security, diagnostics, or payment services, where those providers are actually used. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

The specific providers and their roles depend on the services integrated into the released App. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, address disputes, protect the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is held.
